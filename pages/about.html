About ToolXr

We believe powerful tools should be free, fast, and private for everyone.

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Our Mission

ToolXr exists because we got tired of PDF and image tools that:

  • Upload your private files to unknown servers
  • Hide basic features behind paywalls
  • Require account sign-ups just to compress an image
  • Bombard you with ads and dark patterns

So we built ToolXr — 38 professional tools, 100% free, zero sign-up, privacy-first. Most tools run entirely in your browser, meaning your files never leave your device.

Merge, split, compress, rotate, watermark, OCR, sign and more.

🖼️

10 Image Tools

Our Principles

🔒 Privacy first — Your files stay on your device. We process locally in the browser wherever possible.

⚡ Always free — No freemium tricks. No file size limits. No "premium" version. All 38 tools, free forever.

🚫 No sign-up — Use any tool instantly. No email, no password, no account required.

Technology

ToolXr is built on proven open-source libraries:

  • pdf-lib — Client-side PDF manipulation
  • PDF.js by Mozilla — PDF rendering in the browser
  • Tesseract.js — AI-powered OCR running locally in your browser
  • FileSaver.js — Browser file downloads
  • LibreOffice — Server-side document conversion (Word/Excel/PPT)
  • Node.js + Express — Backend API for conversion tools
